01 — about

A bit about me

I'm Aafrin, a third-year Computer Science and Business Systems student at Aalim Muhammed Salegh College of Engineering, Chennai. I'm still early in my journey as a developer — most of what I know has come from classroom fundamentals, self-paced tutorials, and a growing pile of small personal projects.

Right now I split my time between learning core web technologies, picking up Python and Java for backend and problem-solving, and getting comfortable with tools like Git, SQL, and Django. I've also started exploring AI/ML concepts and large language models, and experimenting with automation tools like n8n out of curiosity.

I enjoy hackathons and student-focused ideas — projects that solve a real, everyday problem for people around me, even if the solution starts small. I'm not claiming expert-level skills anywhere yet; I'm a fresher who likes building, breaking things, and figuring out how they work.
